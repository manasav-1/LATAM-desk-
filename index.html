How each number is calculated

Offramp (USDT → fiat, NGN offramp block): Payout in fiat = Volume × Client rate. Profit in fiat = E×I − E×K. Profit USD = N ÷ K. BPS = Profit USD ÷ Volume, shown as %. Revenue Books = ((Partner rate − Xe) ÷ Xe) × Volume, same as Processing fee. GM% over revenue = O ÷ Q. Processing fee = (Partner rate×Vol − Xe×Vol) ÷ Xe. Partner fee = (Client rate×Vol − Xe×Vol) ÷ Xe. Gross margin = S − T. % = (F − I) ÷ F.

Onramp (fiat → USDT, GHS onramp block): Fiat received = E×J. Payout in fiat = Volume × Client rate. Revenue in fiat = E×L − E×J. Profit USD = O ÷ L. BPS = Profit USD ÷ Volume, shown as %. Revenue Books = ((Partner rate − Xe) ÷ Xe) × Volume, same as Processing fee. Processing fee = (Partner rate×Vol − Xe×Vol) ÷ Xe. Partner fee = (Client rate×Vol − Xe×Vol) ÷ Xe. Gross margin = Processing fee − Partner fee. The onramp sign follows the source sheet; please confirm it with your team.

Swap and payout costs are entered as a percentage of volume (0.04 means 0.04%). Swap cost (USD) = Volume × Swap % ÷ 100 and is added to Profit in USD. Payout cost (USD) = Volume × Payout % ÷ 100 and is deducted from Profit in USD. BPS and GM% over Revenue use this adjusted profit.
